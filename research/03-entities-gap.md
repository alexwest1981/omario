## 5. Bowser and castle completion

Bowser patrols the bridge, jumps and breathes fire; in World 1-4 he is a decoy rather than the final real Bowser. Fire Mario defeats the decoy after five fireball hits; otherwise Mario goes under him during a jump or over him, then grabs the axe. [source: https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/nes/525243-super-mario-bros/faqs/66598] [source: https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/nes/525243-super-mario-bros/faqs/54461]

The original first-castle sequence is: hit Bowser five times with fireballs or pass him; touch the axe; bridge falls and Bowser is defeated; Mario proceeds into the final chamber; a Mushroom Retainer says, “Thank you Mario! But our princess is in another castle!” [source: https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/nes/525243-super-mario-bros/faqs/66598] [source: https://www.world-of-nintendo.com/manuals/nes/super_mario_bros.shtml]

Repository behavior differs: `level4()` places Bowser at column 156 and axe at 161; `reset()` sets Bowser patrol bounds ±2 columns and `vx=-T.enemySpeed*0.6`; `substep()` only translates him horizontally, has no jump/fire/health/fireball hit logic, and `overlap` calls `hurt`. Touching axe immediately sets `status="won"`, clears Bowser, and uses `WON_MSG="THANK YOU MARIO!"`; there is no bridge collapse, chamber/Toad scene, or World 1 “another castle” message. `TITLE_MSG` is a different message (“THE PRINCESS IS IN ANOTHER CASTLE!”) shown before play. [source: Mario.js] [reasoning]

## 6. Repository gap list (primary deliverable)

### Already present

- **World data:** `level1()` to `level4()`, `LEVELS`, and `build()` supply four courses, static ground/pipes/blocks/stairs/coins, Goomba column lists, and a castle Bowser plus axe. World 1-1 data has only Goombas among enemy entities; no Koopas or Paratroopas. [source: Mario.js] [reasoning]
- **Goomba:** `spawnEnemy()`, `T.enemySpeed=34`, collision-based reversal in `substep()`, edge clamp/reversal, falling removal, stomp squash state/timer, and block-bump defeat. `Art.js` has `GOOMBA`, `gWalk1/gWalk2/gSquash`, and `goomba(e)`. [source: Mario.js] [source: Art.js] [reasoning]
- **Mario small/big:** `reset()`, `grow()`, `hurt()`, `kill()`, movement constants in `T`, `overlap()`, `moveX()`, and `moveY()` implement two sizes, hitbox height changes, acceleration/braking, variable jump while held, enemy damage, and death. `Art.js` has `MARIO_SMALL`, `MARIO_BIG`, `mario(m)`. [source: Mario.js] [source: Art.js] [reasoning]
- **Mushroom and coins:** `spawnMushroom()`, `spawnCoin()`, `spawnCoinPop()`, `bumpBlock()`, item update in `substep()`, and `Art.item()`/coin/mushroom frames. `?` and `M` blocks become `U`; `b` is a coin brick; big Mario breaks `B`. [source: Mario.js] [source: Art.js] [reasoning]
- **Bowser/axe approximation:** `reset()` creates Bowser; `substep()` patrols and harms on contact; axe pickup ends game. `Art.js` has Bowser/axe pixel rectangles, `bowser(b)`, `item(it)`. [source: Mario.js] [source: Art.js] [reasoning]
- **Lava appearance only:** `Art.lava()` paints it; level4 decor places lava tiles at gaps. It is not an entity/collision surface. [source: Mario.js] [source: Art.js] [reasoning]
